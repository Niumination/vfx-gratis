## 🎬 **SEKUEN VFX: 12 FASE AWAKAN

### Fase 1: ENTRANCE / DATA STREAM
- Particle system: data stream digital masuk ke humanoid
- Hexagonal particles (cyber/data pattern)
- Flow: binary → encrypted → activation signal

### Fase 2: CORE ACTIVATION
- Energy burst dari "power core" di chest/torso
- Glow: cyan/blue energy
- Shockwave ring

### Fase 3: NEURAL BOOT
- Scan lines over eyes/sensors
- Neural network visualization (nodes/edges)
- Brain activity pulses

### Fase 4: LIMB ACTIVATION
- Servo motor activation effects
- Joint glow
- Motion blur on first movement

### Fase 5: SYSTEM DIAGNOSTICS
- HUD overlay: health/energy/status
- Scan sweep

### Fase 6: AWAKENING COMPLETE
- Full energy surge
- Camera push-in
- Bloom/glow peak
